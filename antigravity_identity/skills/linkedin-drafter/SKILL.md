# LinkedIn Drafter (Alex Hormozi Style)

Generate high-retention, high-engagement LinkedIn posts based on web research and punchy storytelling.

## 3-Step Execution Workflow

### 1. Research the Topic
- Search the web for recent facts, counter-intuitive data, and industry shifts regarding the target keyword.
- Find 1 concrete number, contrast, or lesson.

### 2. Internalize the Alex Hormozi Style
- **Headline / Hook**: 1 sentence that challenges conventional wisdom or states a bold truth.
- **Short lines**: 1-2 sentences per paragraph max.
- **Contrast**: "Most people do X. The top 1% do Y."
- **Framework**:
  - Hook (Problem/Belief)
  - Friction / The realization
  - The simple fix / Rule
  - Punchline / CTA
- **Word count**: Strictly under 150 words.

### 3. Output Format Example

```text
Coding is changing fast.

I just watched Google's new Antigravity platform.
It's an AI-first IDE where autonomous agents do the heavy lifting.

Most devs think better AI results come from better prompts.
They don't.
They come from better systems.

3 things that actually matter:
1. Directives over prompts (tell them WHAT, not how)
2. Deterministic execution (code beats chat)
3. Self-healing loops (fix errors automatically)

Stop writing 50-line prompts.
Build 3-layer systems.
```
