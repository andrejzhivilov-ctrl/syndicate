SYNDICATE

SYNDICATE is a private, single-user system of AI assistants run by its owner for his own training, notes and household work. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

When the owner connects Google Drive, the assistant asks only for the drive.file permission: it can see and change only the files it creates itself in the owner's Drive, and nothing else.
