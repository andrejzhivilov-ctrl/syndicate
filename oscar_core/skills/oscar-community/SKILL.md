# oscar-community

Choose a persona, then use reply or newsletter. Keep replies useful, empathetic and free of abuse; CTA is optional and limited. Never reply automatically or send email. Use queue/report for actual local draft counts. Lead, subscriber, open-rate and conversion metrics remain unavailable until supplied by a verified provider. Read docs/DIRECTOR_SKILLS_CATALOG.md for pinned research, copywriting and community candidates.
